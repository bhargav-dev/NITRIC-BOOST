What Nitric Boost Ultra Actually Contains

Nitric Boost Ultra lists 9 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • L-Arginine — An amino acid widely used in nitric oxide formulas because the body can convert it into nitric oxide, the signaling molecule that helps blood vessels relax and widen.
  • L-Citrulline — Another amino acid that the body recycles into L-arginine, making it a popular partner ingredient in flow-support formulas aimed at workout and everyday stamina.
  • Beet Root Extract — Beetroot is naturally rich in dietary nitrates, which the body converts into nitric oxide, and is one of the most recognizable ingredients in the blood-flow supplement category.
  • L-Citrulline Malate — A buffered form of citrulline often chosen in men's performance formulas for its role in supporting nitric oxide production and exercise tolerance.
  • Pine Bark Extract — A plant extract rich in polyphenols, used in circulation-oriented formulas to help protect nitric oxide from oxidative breakdown.
  • L-Carnitine — An amino acid derivative involved in transporting fatty acids for energy, commonly included in men's vitality and performance blends.
  • Grape Seed Extract — A polyphenol-rich extract traditionally paired with flow-support ingredients for its antioxidant properties and its role in supporting healthy vessel function.
  • Vitamin C — A well-known antioxidant vitamin that helps protect cells from oxidative stress and supports normal collagen formation in blood vessels.

How It Is Meant To Work

By supplying nitric oxide precursors like L-arginine, L-citrulline and beetroot nitrates — and protecting that nitric oxide with antioxidant polyphenols — the formula is designed to help blood vessels relax so circulation, energy and physical stamina can be supported day to day.

Fuel nitric oxide production: L-arginine, L-citrulline and the dietary nitrates in beetroot give your body the raw material it uses to produce nitric oxide, the molecule that signals blood vessels to relax.

Support vessel relaxation: When vessels relax and widen, blood moves more freely, which is the mechanism behind the circulation and stamina support this formula is built around.

Protect your nitric oxide: Grape seed, pine bark and vitamin C act as antioxidants, helping reduce oxidative stress that can otherwise degrade nitric oxide before it does its job.

Feed energy metabolism: Niacin and L-carnitine support normal energy-yielding metabolism, so the formula works alongside your diet and training rather than replacing them.
