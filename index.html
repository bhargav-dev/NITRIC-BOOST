What Are The Ingredients In Nitric Boost Ultra?

Nitric Boost Ultra is formulated with 9 named ingredients — including L-Arginine, L-Citrulline, Beet Root Extract, L-Citrulline Malate, Pine Bark Extract — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

  • 1

    L-Arginine

    NITRIC OXIDE PRECURSOR

    An amino acid widely used in nitric oxide formulas because the body can convert it into nitric oxide, the signaling molecule that helps blood vessels relax and widen.

  • 2

    L-Citrulline

    NITRIC OXIDE PRECURSOR

    Another amino acid that the body recycles into L-arginine, making it a popular partner ingredient in flow-support formulas aimed at workout and everyday stamina.

  • 3

    Beet Root Extract

    DIETARY NITRATES

    Beetroot is naturally rich in dietary nitrates, which the body converts into nitric oxide, and is one of the most recognizable ingredients in the blood-flow supplement category.

  • 4

    L-Citrulline Malate

    PERFORMANCE SUPPORT

    A buffered form of citrulline often chosen in men's performance formulas for its role in supporting nitric oxide production and exercise tolerance.

  • 5

    Pine Bark Extract

    ANTIOXIDANT SUPPORT

    A plant extract rich in polyphenols, used in circulation-oriented formulas to help protect nitric oxide from oxidative breakdown.

  • 6

    L-Carnitine

    ENERGY METABOLISM

    An amino acid derivative involved in transporting fatty acids for energy, commonly included in men's vitality and performance blends.

  • 7

    Grape Seed Extract

    BLOOD VESSEL SUPPORT

    A polyphenol-rich extract traditionally paired with flow-support ingredients for its antioxidant properties and its role in supporting healthy vessel function.

  • 8

    Vitamin C

    ANTIOXIDANT

    A well-known antioxidant vitamin that helps protect cells from oxidative stress and supports normal collagen formation in blood vessels.

  • 9

    Niacin

    CIRCULATION SUPPORT

    A B vitamin that supports normal blood flow and energy-yielding metabolism, often included in nitric oxide blends for its flushing, circulation-related effect.
